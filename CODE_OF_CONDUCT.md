# Code of Conduct

YATSS is a practical engineering project for people working around software,
electronics, race operations, and physical track hardware. Keep discussions
respectful, specific, and useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be patient with people who are testing on different hardware, operating
  systems, and experience levels.
- Give feedback about code, documentation, circuits, and procedures rather than
  personal judgments.
- Describe risks clearly, especially anything involving track power, relay
  wiring, controller watchdog behavior, or data loss.
- Remove private racer names, local paths, credentials, databases, reports, and
  unnecessary serial-log detail before posting.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or repeated unwanted attention.
- Dismissing safety concerns without technical review.
- Posting private data, secrets, or personally identifying logs.
- Pressuring maintainers or testers for unpaid support, releases, or hardware
  validation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may close issues or block
participants when behavior makes the project less safe or less useful. If you
need to report conduct concerns, use the maintainer contact path listed in the
repository security policy.
